### Retake

The **Retake** option chooses what gets regenerated:

| Option | Video | Audio |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Video & Audio** (default) | Gaps and partially masked frames are filled | Regenerated |
| **Video** | Gaps and partially masked frames are filled | Held as is — and can drive the generation |
| **Audio** | **Not** inpainted | Regenerated — essentially *foley mode* |
