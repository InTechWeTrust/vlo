### What comes back

The workflow returns a **pre-masked, positioned clip**: a patch that already
knows where it belongs. Use **Send to Timeline** — in the generation panel, or
from the asset panel's three-dot menu — to drop it straight back where it came
from. Dragging the clip will disable any masks and positions transforms when placing it on the timeline.

The attached mask stays switchable: turn it off or on at any time from the
**mask panel**. Likewise for position transforms, from the **Adjust → Display** tab.
